Research Directions at (Trust & Application AI Lab)

My research focuses on AI for Science (AI4Sci), aiming to connect AI-driven drug discovery (AIDD) with embodied robotic manipulation in a scientific discovery loop. Computational models guide experimental design, while evidence from physical experiments informs model refinement and the next round of research.
